Stack Assignment Notes

Prompt: Describe real-world applications that require stacks as a data structure, explain why stacks are necessary, and choose one application to develop.

1. Real-World Applications

  • Undo/Redo: Reversing text edits in editors or graphics software.
  • Browser History: Back/Forward navigation stack.
  • Call Stack: Managing function calls in programming.
  • Cafeteria Tray Dispenser: Stacking and dispensing food trays.

2. Why Stacks Are Necessary

  • LIFO Principle: Last-In, First-Out guarantees the most recent item is handled first.
  • Reversibility: Essential for undo operations in $O(1)$ time.
  • Chronological Tracking: Maintains nested sequences cleanly without complex indexing.

3. Developed Application

  • Cafeteria Tray Dispenser: Visually simulates pushing clean trays onto the top of the stack and popping them off one by one for customers.
